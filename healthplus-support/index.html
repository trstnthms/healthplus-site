Need help with HealthPlus?

If something is not working, if you have a feature request, or if you want to report a bug, get in touch and I will take a look.

Contact

Email: t@tristanthomas.me

Typical response time: within 3 business days.

Helpful details to include

  • Your device model, Apple Watch model if relevant, and iOS or watchOS version.
  • What you were trying to do in the app.
  • What happened instead.
  • Whether the issue involved Health access, imported files, widgets, Watch, complications, or sleep data.
  • Screenshots if they help explain the issue.

About HealthPlus

HealthPlus helps you see total expenditure, view missing Health stats, compare Health against imported expenditure data, track sleep rolling averages, and use widgets, Apple Watch, and complications.
