HealthPlus

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

HealthPlus is a personal health and expenditure companion app created by Tristan Thomas. This policy explains what information the app processes and how it is used.

Health data

If you grant permission, HealthPlus reads selected data from Apple Health, including Active Energy, Resting Energy, Move goal, workouts, body mass, and sleep. HealthPlus uses this data to calculate and display expenditure trends, activity needed, widgets, Apple Watch views, complications, and sleep rolling averages.

HealthPlus requests read-only Health access. It does not write data to Apple Health.

Files you import

If you import MacroFactor files, HealthPlus processes those files on your device to show summaries and comparisons inside the app.

Optional Dry connection

If you choose to connect Dry, that app asks you to approve sharing the dates you marked Dry or Drank. HealthPlus reads the shared copy on the same device and compares it with your authorised sleep data to show Drinking Impact comparisons. This does not send Health data back to Dry or upload your Dry history to the HealthPlus server.

You can disconnect in either app to stop sharing and clear the shared copy. Your original tracking history remains in Dry.

How your data is used

HealthPlus uses your data to:

  • Show total, active, and resting calorie expenditure.
  • Compare Health expenditure against imported MacroFactor expenditure data.
  • Calculate minimum calories today and activity needed.
  • Track sleep rolling averages.
  • Keep iPhone widgets, Lock Screen widgets, Apple Watch, and complications up to date.

Storage and syncing

HealthPlus stores app settings and calculated display data locally on your device and in shared app-group storage for its widgets.

If you use the Apple Watch app, HealthPlus sends a snapshot of supported calorie and sleep history, your calorie goal and body mass when available, and Drinking Impact comparisons to your paired Watch through Apple's device communication services. This lets the Watch app and complications display your history and comparisons.

Apple Health data and MacroFactor imports are not uploaded to the HealthPlus server. Health exports are created temporarily on your device and removed after you close the share sheet.

Optional parkrun, Strava, and Wallet features

If you choose to connect Strava, HealthPlus sends your Strava athlete ID, encrypted OAuth credentials, parkrun athlete ID and event, result details, description template, candidate Strava activities, and sync status to the HealthPlus service. The service uses this information only to match your public parkrun result to your Strava activity and update the activity description you selected.

If you create a parkrun Wallet pass, the service also stores the pass runner and event details. Apple Wallet sends a device registration identifier and push token to the service so the pass can be refreshed.

For background parkrun result checks, the iPhone app registers an Apple push token and its push-service environment with the HealthPlus service, linked to your Strava connection. The service uses this registration to ask the app to check for new results in the background. The app reports the result date, Strava activity identifier and check outcome, with diagnostic details when needed.

Accounts, analytics, and advertising

HealthPlus does not require you to create an account. HealthPlus does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. A server-side connection record is created only if you connect Strava.

Data sharing

HealthPlus does not sell your personal data. Apple platform services may process data as needed to provide Health, widget, Apple Watch, Wallet, and operating system functionality. If you enable the optional connected features, Cloudflare hosts the HealthPlus service and Strava processes the account and activity requests needed to provide them.

Deleting your data

You can remove local app data by deleting HealthPlus from your device. You can manage Health permissions at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings. You can disconnect Strava or delete the connected-service data from the account page. This removes stored credentials, parkrun details, sync history, Wallet pass records, and Wallet device registrations associated with your parkrun ID. Deleting the connected-service data also removes the app's background push registrations. A pass already saved in Apple Wallet remains on your device until you remove it there.
